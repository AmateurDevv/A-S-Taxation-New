FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does tax return processing take?
+
Most individual tax returns are processed and lodged with the ATO within 24–48 hours of receiving your documents. Once lodged, the ATO typically processes refunds within 2 weeks for e-filed returns.
What documents are required for my tax return?
+
You'll typically need your PAYG payment summaries, bank interest statements, private health insurance details, receipts for work-related deductions, and your TFN. We'll provide a personalised checklist when you book.
Do you support small businesses and sole traders?
+
Absolutely! We specialise in small business, sole trader, and startup taxation. From BAS lodgements to full bookkeeping and annual tax returns — we've got you covered.
Can you help with overdue or late tax returns?
+
Yes — we specialise in catching up overdue returns. Our team can lodge multiple years of overdue returns and can communicate with the ATO on your behalf to minimise any penalties.
Do you offer virtual or online consultations?
+
Yes! We offer fully virtual consultations via Zoom, Teams, or phone. You can also upload documents securely online, so we can assist clients anywhere in Australia.
What are your fees for tax return preparation?
+
Our fees are transparent and competitive. The returns vary based on complexity. Contact us for a free quote tailored to your situation.
